# LinkHub Design Ideas

## Selected Approach: Neo-Brutalist Red & Tan

**Design Movement**: Neo-Brutalism with a warm, earthy palette

**Core Principles**:
- Raw, unpolished aesthetic with intentional "roughness"
- Heavy black borders (3-4px) on all interactive elements
- Bold, chunky typography with strong visual weight
- High contrast between elements with hard shadows
- No rounded corners - sharp edges only (or minimal rounding)

**Color Philosophy**: 
- **Primary Red**: #E63946 (bold, attention-grabbing accent)
- **Tan/Cream Background**: #F4E8D3 (warm, paper-like base)
- **Dark Tan/Brown**: #D4A574 (secondary accent)
- **Black**: #1A1A1A (borders, text, shadows)
- **Off-White**: #FFFEF9 (card backgrounds)

The emotional intent is bold, confident, and unapologetically raw—like a punk zine meets modern web design.

**Layout Paradigm**:
- Stacked cards with visible offset shadows
- Slightly rotated elements for dynamic tension
- Asymmetric spacing that feels intentional
- Grid-breaking layouts with overlapping elements

**Signature Elements**:
- Thick black borders on everything
- Hard drop shadows (no blur, offset 4-8px)
- Uppercase headings with tight letter-spacing
- Underline accents in red
- Hover states that shift shadow position

**Interaction Philosophy**:
Interactions should feel physical and tactile. Buttons "press down" by shifting their shadow. Hover reveals the "weight" of elements through shadow movement.

**Animation**:
- Entrance: Elements slide in with a slight bounce/overshoot
- Hover: Shadow shifts from bottom-right to indicate "press"
- Click: Element moves down into its shadow
- Subtle rotation on hover for playfulness

**Typography System**:
- Display: Space Mono or similar monospace (raw, technical feel)
- Body: Inter or system font (readable contrast)
- All caps for headings with letter-spacing
- Bold weights throughout
